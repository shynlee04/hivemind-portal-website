## 2. Five Deep Modules & Seams

1. **`LocaleDictionaryModule` (`src/modules/locale-dictionary/index.ts`):** Enforces 100% key parity between English (`en`) and Vietnamese (`vi` `{CHINGCHONGCHINGVIBECODING}` "Pro, Not Bro" tone) via Zod.
2. **`WokReactorStageModule` (`src/modules/wok-reactor-stage/index.tsx`):** Three.js 3D Cyber-Wok Reactor (`Devin · swe-2-max`, `Antigravity · argon-4-high`, `Freebuff · glm-5.3-flash / deepseek-4.1-flash`, and the hexagonal `Substrate Honeycomb` base) with automatic fallback to `DeterministicCanvasFallbackAdapter` (`renderDeterministicFallbackSvg`) on SSR or `prefers-reduced-motion`.
3. **`EcosystemCatalogModule` (`src/modules/ecosystem-catalog/index.ts`):** Maps all 20 `< 2-minute` clips (`S1`–`S10`, `M1`–`M9`, `C1`) and the `5-minute` 3-Harness Greenfield build (`V1`–`V5`) to their 3-Layer X-Ray (`Human Prompt -> Orchestrator Tool -> Engine Execution`) and bilingual `"Pro, Not Bro"` Pass-Down Lesson.
4. **`PartnerConversionModule` (`src/modules/partner-conversion/index.ts`):** Centralizes verified partner referrals ([Freebuff Referral](https://freebuff.com/?ref=ref-6c980d54-a29a-4270-a160-35f13e62a2ff), [Zap GLM](https://z.ai/subscribe?ic=LTWUT4H2RS)), community vaults ([Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/shynlee04), [Buy Me a Coffee](https://buymeacoffee.com/shynlee04l)), B2B sponsor tiers, and the deterministic Quota-Savings Calculator.
5. **`ContentShowroomModule` (`src/modules/content-showroom/index.ts`):** Bridges Payload CMS 3.0 localized collections (`episodes`, `articles`, `showcase-slices`, `sponsors`) and Storybook 8 (`<ShowcaseHarness />`).
